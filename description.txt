Robot Jack

Find the Robot Trigger and transform into a sci-fi combat robot.

- Robot Trigger: found in underground Gold Chests, or rarely dropped by enemies underground. Use it to become Robot Jack; use it again to turn back.
- While transformed you get ability items:
  - Orbital Cannon Strike: click anywhere on screen to call down a massive laser from a satellite in orbit.
  - Plasma Cannon: rapid-fire arm cannon.
  - Missile Barrage: a volley of homing micro-missiles.
  - Thruster Dash: rocket toward the cursor, burning everything in the way.
  - Energy Absorber: hold to neutralise attacks, pull enemies in and drain them; right click to release it all as a blast dealing 10x the stored energy.
  - Gravity Arm: hold to pull enemies toward your cursor.
- Robot Jetpack: lets you fly. While transformed, ramming things with your robot head knocks them away spinning.
- Titan forms (Hardmode): Titan Speaker, Titan Camera and Titan TV. Giant forms, each with three abilities of its own: sound waves and bass shockwaves, a core laser and stunning camera flash, hypnotic waves and energy blades.
- Abilities grow stronger as you beat bosses.
